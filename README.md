The Unlabeled Tape
  
_________________

You work the night shift alone at a 2000s video rental store. Every night at closing some figure gives you an unlabeled tape. You play the tape and every night for 5 nights something new gets added to the tape, it feels eerily familiar.

_________________

GAMEPLAY LOOP

You close the store

You see a VHS tape on the counter

Go to back room and play the tape

You get put in the tape and explore

Tape ends. Night is over. Start over next day
